## Unverified items and design decisions
- “Freshmen Beauty Salon” is the user's requested presentation spelling; exact listing spelling is recorded above and used in the contact map area.
- No reliably matched official website/social profile or licensed original logo was located. An original typographic fm wordmark and ivory/charcoal/copper palette were created as design concepts, not represented as the existing official branding.
- The specific service list was not available in the exact listing inspected. Haircuts & styling, beard & grooming, colour & hair care, and skin & self-care are illustrative enquiry categories, clearly labelled on the page. No branch's service list was substituted as verified fact.
- WhatsApp registration for the listed phone number is unverified. Link integration is implemented with a call fallback and clear disclosure.
- No service prices, staff qualifications, awards, business age, customer counts or testimonials are asserted.
- Maps photos are publicly visible but reuse permission was not established. None were copied into the site. All site photography is licensed Pexels stock, labelled and credited.
